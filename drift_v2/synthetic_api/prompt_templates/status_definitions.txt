Anxiety: persistent worry, tension, restlessness, panic, racing thoughts, fear, or physical anxiety symptoms.
Bipolar: alternating elevated and depressed mood, reduced need for sleep, impulsive energy, or unstable mood episodes.
Depression: sadness, emptiness, hopelessness, low energy, loss of interest, guilt, or withdrawal.
Normal: no meaningful mental health complaint, stable mood, ordinary daily concerns, or generally well state.
Personality disorder: unstable relationships, identity disturbance, intense reactions, chronic interpersonal conflict, or rigid maladaptive patterns.
Stress: overload from work, study, family, or life pressure with tension and burnout-like complaints.
Suicidal: explicit wish to die, self-harm intent, hopelessness tied to death, or thoughts of ending life.
