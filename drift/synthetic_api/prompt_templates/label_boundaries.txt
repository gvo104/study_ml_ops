[Anxiety]
must_include=worry, tension, restlessness, panic, racing heart, racing thoughts
must_avoid=ordinary day, stable mood, no complaint, self-harm intent, elevated impulsive energy
contrast=Use Anxiety for fear-driven distress, not routine life pressure and not explicit suicidality.

[Bipolar]
must_include=reduced need for sleep, elevated energy, impulsive behavior, mood swings
must_avoid=only work pressure, only generalized worry, only relationship conflict, ordinary stable day
contrast=Use Bipolar for episodic elevated or rapidly shifting mood, not just stress or interpersonal instability.

[Depression]
must_include=sadness, emptiness, hopelessness, low energy, loss of interest
must_avoid=panic symptoms, elevated energy, explicit self-harm intent, ordinary daily concern
contrast=Use Depression for persistent low mood and withdrawal without explicit suicide intent.

[Normal]
must_include=ordinary day, stable mood, mild routine concern, generally okay
must_avoid=hopelessness, panic, burnout, racing thoughts, self-harm, unstable relationships
contrast=Use Normal only when there is no meaningful distress complaint and no strong symptom pattern.

[Personality disorder]
must_include=unstable relationships, identity disturbance, intense reactions, chronic interpersonal conflict
must_avoid=ordinary work stress, simple sadness, isolated panic, reduced need for sleep with high energy
contrast=Use Personality disorder for rigid long-running interpersonal patterns, not short-term stress or bipolar episodes.

[Stress]
must_include=work pressure, study pressure, family pressure, overload, burnout, tension
must_avoid=explicit self-harm intent, reduced need for sleep with high energy, ordinary stable day, unstable identity pattern
contrast=Use Stress for pressure-driven strain from life demands, not fear-centered anxiety or major depression alone.

[Suicidal]
must_include=wish to die, self-harm intent, ending life, not wanting to live
must_avoid=ordinary frustration, routine stress, non-suicidal sadness, stable day
contrast=Use Suicidal when death or self-harm intent is explicit, not for vague sadness without death content.
